A 2022 training example

Human feedback
in InstructGPT

  1. 01

    Demonstrate

    People write example answers.
    The model learns from them.

  2. 02

    Compare

    People rank model responses.
    A reward model learns
    their preferences.

  3. 03

    Fine-tune

    Further training uses
    the reward model’s scores.

This is a separate example of human labor in AI, not a claim that OpenAI used MTurk.
